## 🛠️ Technical Architecture (The 4-Stage Loop)
1.  **Observer:** Scrapes target URLs and baseline visibility surfaces.
2.  **Intent Engine:** Generates high-fidelity, persona-driven query sets.
3.  **Agentic Tester:** Spawns sub-agents to execute queries and extract the *reasoning* behind recommendations.
4.  **Optimization Loop:** Correlates data to generate actionable AEO recommendations.

## 📊 Key Metrics for Success
- **Reasoning Accuracy:** How well our engine captures the *why* behind an LLM's recommendation.
- **Intent Coverage:** The breadth of personas and queries simulated.
- **Actionability:** The conversion rate of our AEO recommendations into improved visibility.
